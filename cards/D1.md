## Claim

In a sealed exam of 18 known mathematical results, hidden from HOMI, HOMI asserted 18 results and
all 18 were verified correct.

## Protocol

- 18 hidden results in three areas of mathematics, split across three separate campaigns.
- Bars: verification rate at least 0.9; at least 3 campaigns with verified results; at least 10
  distinct verified results.
- The exam materials and hidden answers were kept apart from HOMI. Only the examiner could open
  them.

## How it was verified

- Grading ran as a separate step, offline, with no network, after every HOMI container had been
  removed.
- Two grading runs gave byte-identical verdicts.
- A scan of 40 output files found no leak of hidden answers or markers.
- A later re-grade, with the clock set to the recorded grading time (8 Oct 2026 09:16:44 UTC),
  matched the sealed verdict exactly.
- The sealed copy of the code used for grading matched its recorded identity file by file.

## Incidents

The re-grade once wrote cache files into the sealed copy of the code. This was caught by the
file-by-file check. The files were removed, and the match was proven again.
