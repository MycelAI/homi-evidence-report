## Claim

HOMI produced a new heuristic for a synthetic online bin-packing workload. On 400 hidden test
instances it used fewer bins than each of the 74 published heuristics tested.

## Protocol

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Task | Online bin packing: place items, one at a time, into as few bins as possible |
| Workload | Synthetic, defined by Mycel UG before the study |
| Test set | 400 hidden instances, sealed before the study |
| Metric | Total bins used (fewer is better) |
| Comparators | 74 published heuristics, fixed before the study |
| Pass bar | At least 0.25% fewer bins than the best comparator |

## Outcome

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Bins used by HOMI's heuristic | 38,807 |
| Bins used by the best comparator | 38,925 |
| Saving | 0.303% (bar 0.25%) |
| Comparators beaten | 74 of 74 |
| Rebuild by a different AI model family | Exact bin assignments on 400 of 400 instances, first of two allowed attempts |

## How it was verified

1. An exact scorer, in a sandbox, re-ran every candidate on the sealed instances and counted bins.
2. A verify step re-checked the frozen code, the budget ledger, every model call against its raw
   record, each campaign ledger, the finalist selection (fixed in advance) and the qualification
   results.
3. That verify step was run again on a copy of the sealed evidence. The result was identical
   (about 8 minutes).
4. Novelty check against the fixed set of published heuristics and against every earlier D2
   candidate.
5. Specification check and adversarial review.
6. A different AI model family rebuilt the heuristic from its written specification alone, with no
   access to its source. The rebuild placed every item in the same bin on all 400 hidden
   instances.

## Limitations

- The workload is synthetic. It is not a public benchmark or customer data.
- New relative to the 74 methods tested and earlier D2 candidates only. The 74 are a fixed set,
  not the whole literature.
- The gain is small (0.3%).
- Three earlier D2 studies on SDK 1.5.3 missed the bar. This was not a first try.
- Not reproduced outside Mycel UG.

## Redacted from this card

The heuristic's name, source, specification, structure, rules, ordering, scoring and parameters;
its size; the workload's parameters; the study design and campaign structure; model names; the
names of the 74 comparators; internal paths; the code commit; the verifier's implementation.
